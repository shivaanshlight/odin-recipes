Pizza

Pizza is a flat, baked bread dough base covered with a savory tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various toppings

No yeast? No problem! This no-yeast pizza dough recipe is not only quicker than traditional dough, it yields perfect homemade pizza crust every time!

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • baking powder
  • salt
  • milk
  • olive oil

Steps to follow

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Bake the crust in the preheated oven for 8 minutes.
  3. Top with your favorite toppings and bake until the crust is golden brown, about 10 to 15 minutes more.
